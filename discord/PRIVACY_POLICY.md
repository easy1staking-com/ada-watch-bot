# Ada Watch Bot Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 12, 2025**

This Privacy Policy explains how Ada Watch Bot ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Ada Watch Bot ("the Bot"), a Discord bot that tracks Cardano wallet activity, DeFi loans on Liqwid Finance, and Collateralized Debt Positions (CDPs) on Indigo Protocol. By using the Bot, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our [Terms of Service](#terms-of-service).

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect minimal information to operate the Bot:

### a. Information You Provide
- **Discord User ID**: To identify you and process commands within Discord.
- **Cardano Wallet Addresses**: Public wallet addresses you submit to track transactions, balances, or DeFi activity.

### b. Automatically Collected Information
- **Blockchain Data**: Publicly available data from the Cardano blockchain (e.g., transactions, balances) retrieved via a Cardano node. This data is not stored.
- **Usage Data**: Basic logs of commands used and timestamps for debugging and performance monitoring.

### c. Non-Personally Identifiable Information
We may aggregate or anonymize data (e.g., usage statistics) that cannot be used to identify you.

**We do not collect or store any other personal information, such as names, email addresses, or private keys.**

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Provide the Bot’s core functionality, such as tracking wallet activity and sending notifications.
- Debug and improve the Bot’s performance.
- Ensure security and prevent abuse.
- Comply with legal obligations under Irish and EU law (e.g., GDPR).

## 3. Data Accuracy and Responsibility
As stated in our Terms of Service, we are not responsible for bad, invalid, incorrect, or malformed data retrieved from the Cardano blockchain, Cardano node, Liqwid Finance, or Indigo Protocol. You are solely responsible for verifying and acting on the data provided by the Bot.

## 4. How We Share Your Information
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, except:
- **With Third-Party Services**:
    - **Discord**: To process commands and deliver responses (subject to Discord’s Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy).
    - **Cardano Node**: To retrieve blockchain data (public and not stored).
    - **Liqwid Finance/Indigo Protocol**: To access DeFi loan or CDP data (subject to their policies).
- **For Legal Reasons**: To comply with applicable laws, respond to legal requests, or protect our rights.
- **In Aggregated Form**: Anonymized data may be used for analytics.

## 5. Blockchain Data
Cardano blockchain data (e.g., wallet addresses, transactions) is public and pseudonymous, typically not considered personal data under GDPR. We retrieve this data in real-time via a Cardano node and do not store it.

## 6. Data Storage and Security
- **Storage**: We store only your Discord user ID and Cardano wallet addresses in a secure, encrypted database in EU-based servers.
- **Security**: We use industry-standard measures (e.g., encryption, access controls) to protect stored data. However, no system is completely secure, and you share data at your own risk.
- **Retention**: We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the Bot’s services or comply with legal requirements (e.g., GDPR). Data is deleted upon request or when you remove the Bot.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR and Irish Law)
Under GDPR and Irish data protection law, you may have rights to:
- Access, correct, or delete your personal information (i.e., Discord user ID, wallet addresses).
- Object to or restrict data processing.
- Request data portability.
  To exercise these rights, contact us at info.easy1staking@gmail.com. Note that blockchain data cannot be modified or deleted due to its immutable nature.

## 8. Data Deletion
To stop data collection, remove the Bot from your Discord server. To request deletion of stored data (Discord user ID, wallet addresses), email info.easy1staking@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days, as required by GDPR.

## 9. Third-Party Services
The Bot relies on:
- **Discord**: Subject to Discord’s Privacy Policy.
- **Cardano Node**: Public blockchain data, no additional privacy policy applies.
- **Liqwid Finance and Indigo Protocol**: Subject to their privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the practices of these third parties.

## 10. International Data Transfers
Your data (Discord user ID, wallet addresses) is processed in Ireland or the EU. If transferred outside the EU (e.g., to Discord’s US servers), we ensure compliance with GDPR requirements.

## 11. Children’s Privacy
The Bot is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have such data, contact us at info.easy1staking@gmail.com.
